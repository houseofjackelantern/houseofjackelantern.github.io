Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What this app does

House of Jackel Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API.

Data access and use

The app requests the youtube.upload scope, which allows it to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel and manage those uploads. It does not read, collect, or store your personal data beyond what is required to perform uploads.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties. Uploaded videos are published to your own YouTube channel per your settings in the app.

Data retention

The app does not retain copies of your videos or personal information on external servers. You may revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings under Security > Third-party access.
